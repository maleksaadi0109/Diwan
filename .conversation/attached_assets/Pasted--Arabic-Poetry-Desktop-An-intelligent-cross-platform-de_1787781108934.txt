# 📜 Arabic Poetry Desktop (ديوان الشعر العربي)

An intelligent, cross-platform desktop application for discovering, listening to, and understanding Classical Arabic Poetry. The application automatically synchronizes YouTube audio recitations with written poem verses, provides millisecond-precise forced alignment, displays poetic meter and prosodic analysis, and offers in-depth vocabulary explanations from classical lexicons like **Lisan Al-Arab (لسان العرب)**.

---

## 🌟 1. Core Vision & Objectives

1. **Automated Audio-Text Forced Alignment**:
   * Given a YouTube recitation and a poem text URL (from Mizan Al-Arab or AlDiwan), automatically detect verse timestamps with zero manual tagging.
   * Intelligently strip introductory silence, video title screens, background commentary, and outro music.
2. **Immersive Audio Player & Synchronized Typography**:
   * Beautiful RTL Arabic typography (Amiri & Cairo font families) with full Tashkeel (diacritics).
   * Active verse highlighting, smooth auto-scrolling, and interactive seek-to-verse navigation.
3. **Deep Literary Analysis & Classical Lexicons**:
   * Click any verse to view its complete literary meaning, poetic meter (البحر العروضي والتفعيلات), and classical word definitions.
   * Integrated with **Mizan Al-Arab API** and **Lisan Al-Arab** lexicon.
4. **Lightweight Native Desktop Experience**:
   * Built on **Tauri + Rust + React + Vite**, offering native performance, instant startup, and tiny memory footprint compared to Electron.

## 🏗️ 3. System Architecture & Alignment Pipeline

```mermaid
graph TD
    A["YouTube Recitation URL"] --> B["yt-dlp Audio Extractor"]
    C["Mizan Al-Arab / AlDiwan URL"] --> D["Poem & Lexicon Scraper"]
    
    B --> E["16kHz Mono WAV Audio"]
    E --> F["Voice Activity Detector (VAD)"]
    F -->|Strip Intro/Outro| G["Speech Energy Envelope"]
    
    D --> H["Diacritized Verses & Metadata"]
    H --> I["Arabic Text Normalizer"]
    
    G --> J["Speech Recognition / Energy Peaks"]
    I --> K["Semi-Global Word Aligner (Needleman-Wunsch)"]
    J --> K
    
    K --> L["Verse Boundary Detector"]
    L --> M["Confidence Scorer (0-100%)"]
    M --> N["Audio Segmenter (ffmpeg)"]
    
    N --> O["Per-Verse MP3 Audio Slices"]
    L --> P["Unified Poem JSON Database"]
    
    O --> Q["Interactive Desktop GUI (React + Tauri)"]
    P --> Q
```

### Alignment Pipeline Stages:

1. **`ArabicTextNormalizer`**:
   * Strips Tashkeel, Tatweel (Kashida), and punctuation.
   * Unifies letter variants (`أ/إ/آ/ٱ` $\to$ `ا`, `ى` $\to$ `ي`, `ة` $\to$ `ه`).
   * Computes normalized character Levenshtein similarity ($Sim \ge 0.68$) to accommodate poetic rhyme inflections.

2. **`VoiceActivityDetector` (VAD)**:
   * Evaluates RMS energy envelopes across 16kHz audio frames.
   * Detects true recitation start, stripping silent intros, sponsor graphics, and commentary.
   * Detects recitation end, ignoring outro music and post-poem remarks.

3. **`SemiGlobalWordAligner`**:
   * Dynamic programming with free start and end gaps ($D[0, j] = 0$).
   * Allows arbitrary pre-poem speech (e.g. *"هذه القصيدة للمتنبي..."*) to pass without alignment drift.
   * Tolerates word omissions, speech recognition noise, and insertions.

4. **`VerseBoundaryDetector` & `ConfidenceScorer`**:
   * Maps word token spans to exact timestamps $[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]$.
   * Multi-factor scoring: $0.40 \cdot \text{MatchRatio} + 0.25 \cdot \text{FuzzySim} + 0.20 \cdot P_{\text{asr}} + 0.15 \cdot \text{TimeConsistency}$.
   * Badges verses $\ge 80\%$ (Emerald), $\ge 65\%$ (Amber), or $< 65\%$ (Review).

5. **`AudioSegmenter`**:
   * Cuts standalone per-verse MP3 files (`verse_001.mp3`, `verse_002.mp3`, ...) with anti-overlap midpoint clamping.

## 📚 4. Data Sources & Lexicon Integration

1. **Mizan Al-Arab (`mizanalarab.com`) REST API**:
   * **Poem Data**: `/api/poems/{id}` — Clean verses, poet biographical info, and poetic meter.
   * **Lexicon Glossary**: `/api/glossary/?word={word}` — Full classical definitions from *Lisan Al-Arab (لسان العرب)*, *Al-Sihah (الصحاح)*, and *Ma'jam Al-Ghani (معجم الغني)*.
   * **Prosodic Analysis**: `/api/analysis/` — Metric meter name, Taf'ilat (التفعيلات), and prosodic scansion (التقطيع العروضي).

2. **AlDiwan (`aldiwan.net`)**:
   * Comprehensive archive for pre-Islamic, Umayyad, Abbasid, and Andalusian poems.
   * Community annotations and vocabulary definitions.

3. **YouTube Recitations**:
   * Supports recitations by legendary performers (e.g. Osama El-Waz, Abdulrahman Al-Sabih, Faleh Al-Qudaa).
